The Lore of Project Vritra

The single source of truth behind the conspiracy, ancient manuscripts, and the Ninth Mandala.

The Meaning of "Vritra"

In ancient Indian mythology, Vritra represents the primordial serpent of obstruction—the cosmic obstacle blocking the flow of life and truth. In modern India, Project Vritra is not a person, but an underground institutional conspiracy that manipulates evidence, erases records, and conceals corporate and political crimes behind ancient symbols.

The Sutradhar (Dr. Vedant Kashyap)

The Sutradhar ("The Puppet Master") is the symbolic title given to Dr. Vedant Kashyap. Operating from behind the scenes, his weapon is not physical violence—it is the control, deletion, and fabrication of information. Only an investigator skilled in SQL can query through the falsehoods to recover the truth.

The Ninth Mandala

An ancient secret network whose presence spans across temple ciphers, corporate shell ledgers, automated predictive models (The Maya Protocol & Oracle Network), and government archives. Discovered piece by piece through the 18 active murder cases.

The 4 Canonical Story Arcs

  • Act I — The Pattern (Cases 1–5): Seemingly disconnected homicides reveal identical Naga symbols and rare chemical compounds.
  • Act II — The Conspiracy (Cases 6–10): Uncovering offshore banking ledgers, vanishing witnesses, and the first mentions of Project Vritra.
  • Act III — The System (Cases 11–15): Uncovering institutional corruption, the Seven Shadows, and automated population surveillance.
  • Act IV — The Architect (Cases 16–18): Concluding the investigation by bringing down the Sutradhar and presenting the final indictment.

SQL Core Commands Reference

SELECT * FROM table; — Inspect Table Records

Retrieves all records and fields from a department table (e.g. SELECT * FROM suspects WHERE case_id = 1;).

WHERE column = 'value'; — Filter Suspects & Evidence

Isolates specific records matching parameters (e.g. WHERE status = 'Missing' or WHERE type = 'Chemical').

ORDER BY time ASC; — Chronological Reconstruction

Sorts timeline occurrences sequentially (e.g. ORDER BY time ASC).

JOIN table2 ON table1.id = table2.case_id; — Cross-Reference Proof

Connects suspects to matching forensic laboratory reports and fingerprints.

🏛️ Case 1: The Nagabhavan Estate Mystery (Act I)

Perpetrator: Raghav Sethi

SELECT * FROM suspects WHERE case_id = 1;
SELECT * FROM witnesses WHERE case_id = 1;
SELECT * FROM evidence WHERE case_id = 1;
SELECT * FROM forensics WHERE case_id = 1;
SELECT * FROM timeline WHERE case_id = 1 ORDER BY time ASC;
SELECT s.name, s.motive, f.analysis_result FROM suspects s JOIN forensics f ON s.case_id = f.case_id WHERE s.case_id = 1 AND f.analysis_result LIKE '%Raghav Sethi%';
